SafeWalk · Visual Direction

Rooted in the color of solidarity

Purple carries real meaning in South Africa right now — it's the color of the Women for Change movement against gender-based violence. SafeWalk's palette leans into that deliberately: deep purple for moments of meaning, softer violet for everyday use, and red reserved only for real danger.
